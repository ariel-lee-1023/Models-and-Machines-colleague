- Retained: Verify/Precise/Continue, relay-results and distinction from rapid calculation (2699–2795; 2840–2890). No algorithmic account of the unconscious claimed.
- Retained: synthesis, help of signs, partial schemas, and diversity of representations (2995–3050; 4489–4550; 6050–6190). Full diagram and formula examples excluded because unnecessary and conversion-fragmented.
- Retained: Paradoxical Cases of Intuition as conjecture versus justification; Fermat status explicitly dated to the historical text (5129–5208). No present open-problem survey claimed.
- Appendix I retained as historical inquiry questions, not validated measurement. Appendix II retains Einstein's first-person qualification. Historical testimony from other figures, detailed physiological speculation, and Final Remarks are compressed or excluded; no modern cognitive mechanism inferred.
- Worked example: Poincaré's omnibus account (920–1090), with verification kept distinct from certainty and bus travel not treated as a cause.

Outside-inventory audit:

1. 3590–3650: retained Hadamard's auditory everyday preference alongside visual research schemas and the external expression replacing an internal image. This changes advice: no fixed learning-type matching and no presumption that offloading harms understanding.
2. 5427–5500: retained the two directions of invention—means for an end, and a discovered result finding a use—and the reciprocal role of application in theoretical questions. This prevents a rigid problem-to-solution workflow from swallowing reframing.
